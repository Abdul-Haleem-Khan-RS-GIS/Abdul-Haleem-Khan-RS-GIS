## 🌍 About Me

> *"Turning raw satellite data into spatial intelligence that drives real decisions."*

I am a **GIS Engineer & Remote Sensing Specialist** with **5+ years of experience** delivering geospatial solutions for governments, engineering firms, and international clients. Currently based in **Uyun Al Jawa, Saudi Arabia**, working at the Municipality and as a freelance analyst on Upwork.
