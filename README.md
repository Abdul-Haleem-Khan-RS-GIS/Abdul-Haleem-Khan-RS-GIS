## 👤 About Me

> *"Turning raw satellite data into spatial intelligence that drives real decisions."*

I am a **GIS Engineer & Remote Sensing Specialist** with **3+ years of experience** delivering geospatial solutions for governments, engineering firms, and international clients. Currently based in **Uyun Al Jawa, Saudi Arabia**, serving as a GIS Engineer at the Municipality and as a freelance analyst on Upwork.

I bridge the gap between raw geodata and real decisions - whether that's mapping wildfire burn severity from Sentinel-2 imagery, modeling solar site suitability with MCDA, optimizing urban logistics networks, or delivering publication-quality cartographic outputs.
